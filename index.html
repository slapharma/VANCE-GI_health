GI Health

Find out more about the six GI conditions most prevalent in the UK.

Inflammatory Bowel Disease

The umbrella term for long-term conditions — chiefly Crohn's and ulcerative colitis — that inflame the digestive tract.

Learn more →

Ulcerative Colitis

A form of IBD causing inflammation and ulcers in the lining of the colon and rectum.

Learn more →

Crohn's Disease

A form of IBD that can inflame any part of the gut, from mouth to anus, often the small intestine.

Learn more →

Microscopic Colitis

Inflammation of the colon visible only under a microscope, causing chronic watery diarrhoea.

Learn more → Now covered

Irritable Bowel Syndrome

A common functional disorder causing abdominal pain, bloating and changes in bowel habit.

Colorectal Cancer

Cancer of the colon or rectum — highly treatable when caught early through screening.

Understanding your gut

Digestive conditions are more common than you might think

The gut does far more than digest food — it influences immunity, mood and overall wellbeing. Millions of people live with a digestive condition, and most can be well managed with the right information and care.
